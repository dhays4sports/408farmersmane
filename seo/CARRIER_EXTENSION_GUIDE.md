# Carrier extension guide

1. Verify a meaningful California displacement development with current primary sources.
2. Add the carrier/aliases to the carrier registry without changing the intake engine.
3. Add dated facts to `cf-disp/CARRIER_FACT_REGISTRY.json`, including allowed and forbidden public claims.
4. Keep the carrier campaign inactive until factual/compliance review is complete.
5. If activated, add one genuinely useful carrier-specific page only when search/referral demand supports it.
6. Reuse the six-question intake, operational urgency classifier, checkpoint, PVX continuation, producer record and measurement system.
7. Never generate carrier × city doorway pages or automatically publish from the monitoring feed.
